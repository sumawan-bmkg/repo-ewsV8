6. Operational Readiness

ORL-2

Operational Readiness Level
Bottleneck: Calibration. Recall=23.2% di threshold optimal masih jauh dari target operasional 85%. Model unggul dalam ranking (AUPRC=0.985) namun probabilitas tidak terkalibrasi baik.
Memerlukan Platt Scaling atau Temperature Scaling sebelum deployment.

Strengths

  • Zero false alarm selama badai geomagnetik (Kp≥4)
  • AUPRC=0.985 — ranking sempurna
  • Azimuth MAE=38° — memenuhi target ~45°
  • Precision 0.994 di th optimal
  • Training stabil 35 epoch tanpa divergence
  • 24 stasiun MAGDAS terintegrasi

Limitations

  • Recall rendah (≤25%) akibat calibration gap
  • Magnitude MAE=4.27 Mw — kelas magnitude terlalu coarse
  • Distance MAE=1656 km — station-to-event matching approximate
  • 98.3% class imbalance ekstrem
  • Hanya 48 sampel negatif di blind test
  • No pre-computed GradCAM/XAI
